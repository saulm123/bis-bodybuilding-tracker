Privacy policy

BIS Bodybuilding Tracker · Effective 12 October 2026

Short version: the app does not collect, store or share any of your data on any server. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Food logs, targets, body weight, progress photos, workouts, cardio, blood pressure and heart rate, blood test results, supplements and any other health logs you enter. All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer has no access to it, and there is no account or cloud sync.

The only network requests

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, the app sends that barcode or those search words to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to fetch nutrition information. Nothing else is sent: no identifiers, no logs, no health data. Open Food Facts' handling of requests is covered by their privacy policy. If you don't scan or search, the app makes no network requests at all.

Permissions

  • Camera: to read barcodes (processed on the phone, never saved or sent) and, if you choose, to take progress photos. Progress photos are stored only inside the app on your phone, never uploaded, and only included in a backup file if you tick that option.
  • Notifications: for rest timer, supplement and blood test reminders you switch on. These are scheduled on the phone.
  • Internet: only for the Open Food Facts lookups above.

No tracking

No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, Google Play Services, Firebase or other third-party SDKs are included.

Backups

You can export your data to a file and import it again. That file is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to put it. Keep it somewhere safe, because it contains your health information.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything. Restoring a backup with "Replace" also wipes the existing data first.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

Not medical advice

The app is a log. Reference ranges and suggestions are general and may not suit you. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Changes and contact
