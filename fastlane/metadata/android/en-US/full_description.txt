BIS Bodybuilding Tracker is a fast, offline tracker for people who lift. Everything is stored on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no Google services.

<b>Nutrition</b>
<ul>
<li>Daily calorie, protein, carb and fat targets with a bulk/cut switch</li>
<li>Food search and barcode scanning using the free Open Food Facts database</li>
<li>Saved meals and one-tap favourites</li>
<li>Weight log with a 7-day average trend and calorie adjustment suggestions</li>
</ul>

<b>Training</b>
<ul>
<li>Editable 5-day split with sets, reps and weight logging</li>
<li>Last session's numbers shown next to every exercise</li>
<li>"Add weight" flag when you hit the top of the rep range</li>
<li>Rest timer, deload reminders and cardio log</li>
</ul>

<b>Health</b>
<ul>
<li>Blood pressure and resting heart rate with weekly averages</li>
<li>Blood test tracker with reminders</li>
<li>Daily supplement checklist</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
The only network request the app makes is looking up foods on Open Food Facts. Back up and restore all your data as a JSON file.
